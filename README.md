# Lodestar Backcountry Provisions

### Experiential goods from Canada’s wild places.

We turn field discoveries and backcountry stories into thoughtful goods for kitchens, camps, and curious people.

## Current field release

**Ishpatina Ridge Sourdough Starter — Field Release 001**

A dehydrated wild-yeast sourdough starter connected to an expedition into Ontario’s remote backcountry. Each release brings a place, a culture, and the story of how they came together into your kitchen.

[Explore Field Release 001](https://lodestarprovisions.ca/products/ishpatina-ridge-sourdough-starter)

## What we’re building

This profile is the home for the projects and practical resources behind Lodestar:

- Field releases and the stories behind them
- Experiments in wild fermentation and place-based food
- Useful tools and workflows developed as we grow the brand
- The systems behind our marketing work, documented and shared in stages

We’ll publish each project with clear context, practical instructions, and notes on what we learned—so it’s useful whether you’re a maker, a small brand, or simply curious about what can grow from a journey outdoors.
